Caitlin Shepherd's Biography

Hi, I’m Caitlin Shepherd. I'm a Public Relations major at Syracuse University's S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications, with a concentration in Fashion and Beauty Communications and a minor in Information Management & Technology. What draws me to PR is that it's a field built on patience and consistency, where you invest energy into building relationships that engage a brand's audience and stakeholders over time, shaping how the public understands and connects with that brand's values.

I've had the chance to put that mindset into practice through hands-on experiences throughout the past year. As a Public Relations & Marketing Intern at Cinq à Sept , based in New York City, I supported media tracking, trend research, and pitch development while managing sample logistics and stylist/influencer outreach. At Syracuse, I currently serve as Director of Social Media for Phi Sig, where I grew our Instagram and TikTok accounts significantly. I also lead as Director of Communications for the Fashion and Design Society where I manage communications for our shows and events. Along the way, I've built a versatile skill set spanning Adobe Creative Suite, Canva Pro, Qualtrics, Brandwatch, and social platforms like Instagram and TikTok.
